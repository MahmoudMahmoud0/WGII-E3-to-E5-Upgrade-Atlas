How to read this reference

The five columns in each Electric level are its intermediate upgrade steps. A column ending in E4 or E5 is the final step into that level. Church’s earlier observed upgrades, beginning at level 30, are under Earlier stages.

Costs are required quantities, not resources already owned. Base time is the on-screen “Time Cost”; boosted time is the duration on the Upgrade button. Account bonuses can change between recordings, so different observed values are preserved. “Not shown” means no requirement row was visible; a dash means no readable panel was captured. Missing values have not been extrapolated.

Silver Mine observations share one row; source details identify the mine number. All three camps share requirements using your confirmation. Furnace, Quarry, Farm and Lumber Mill also share requirements; missing entries and unreadable values are filled from another production building. Their final E4 → E5 costs and times were supplied directly by you. Missing camp entries are filled from another camp’s recorded panel, which is identified in the source notes. Your cube table fills remaining gaps; its levels 46–55 are aligned with the ten E3–E5 columns. Where the supplied table differs from a readable video cost, both values are identified and the video reading remains the main value.

Furniture: Workbench, Casting Mold, Crafting Table and Tool Table buttons show prices increasing by 1,000 per furniture level, from 34k for level 51 → 52 through 47k for 64 → 65. No cube requirement was displayed. Their 14s, 24s, 3s and 9s timers are production cycles.
